# Evidence-to-Insight

The study layer turns uploaded study text into auditable descriptive evidence for Ask AI.

- Claims are sentence-level extracted observations.
- Polarity is heuristic and descriptive, not statistical.
- Topics are heuristic FMCG signals.
- Provenance identifies the uploaded study and extraction method.
- Contradictions are potential conflicts requiring human review.
- Removing studies removes their evidence context.
