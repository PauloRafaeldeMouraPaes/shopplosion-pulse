# Category-first scope — Chocolate pilot

## Decision principle

The unit of discovery is the **chocolate category and its shopper/market context**, not a single brand's approved product list. The first commercial user may be Lindt, but Lindt does not define the universe of signals and does not need to approve category-level discovery.

Use broad category and need-state signals first; use brands and SKUs as optional comparison dimensions where evidence supports them. Do not make brand coverage a prerequisite for starting the shopper-side discovery stream.

## Signal layers

1. **Shopper language and needs:** category terms, use occasions, gifting, ingredients, dietary needs, texture/flavor, premiumization/value and emerging product formats. Treat Trends as relative search interest, not purchase behavior.
2. **Culture and public conversation:** dated public articles, recipes, reviews and public discussions, subject to each source's access terms, collection rules and citation requirements. Deduplicate syndicated stories and distinguish original reporting from repetition.
3. **Retail offer and assortment:** prices per 100 g, pack sizes, promotional mechanics, availability and assortment across a representative multi-brand basket and more than one retailer/network when feasible. Product/EAN matching is a QA method, not a brand-approval gate.
4. **Industry and supply context:** production, commodity inputs, seasonality and sector reports. Keep these separate from direct shopper observations.
5. **Brand/SKU diagnostics:** optional drill-down after a category signal is detected. Do not imply proprietary access, market share, sell-out, preference or switching without suitable evidence.

## Sampling rule

Freeze the initial category taxonomy, queries and sampling method before inspecting the resulting curves. The retail basket should be selected by transparent coverage criteria (format, weight range, price tier, product type and retailer availability), not by a target brand's approval. Record all included and excluded items, coverage gaps and why the sample is or is not representative. Expand the basket when category coverage requires it.

Category discovery should begin with openly accessible public signals and should not depend on buyer, brand, retailer or supplier approval. This does not mean every item online can be copied, scraped or redistributed without limits; use reasonable access methods, attribute sources, avoid personal/sensitive data and do not bypass access controls. Paid feeds are optional enrichment, not a prerequisite.

## Interpretation

- State facts with source, date and universe; keep hypothesis separate.
- Seek independent corroboration across source families, not merely multiple publishers repeating one source.
- Label single-source findings preliminary.
- Never infer purchases, sell-out, preference, elasticity or brand switching from search interest, shelf prices, recipe trends or sector totals alone.
- A four-week baseline starts when the first real, reproducible, QA-accepted category capture is available; it does not wait for a brand-specific product list.
